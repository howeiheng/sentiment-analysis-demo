## What is Sentiment Analysis?

Sentiment analysis is the process of determining whether a piece of text is positive, negative, or neutral. This demo uses artificial intelligence to automatically analyze the emotion or opinion in any text you provide.

## Why use Machine Learning and not Generative AI/LLMs (Chat GPT, Claude, Gemini)?

Gen AI is very powerful, and it is more than capable of performing sentiment analysis on text. It is able to understand context very well, and is able to pull out a lot more information from text than most machine learning models. If you require labels beyond positive or negative, gen AI is able to generate much more nuanced labels such as "unhappy but polite", "sarcastically positive", "sincerely grateful" etc. 

Machine learning is much more straightforward. Train the model (or use a pre-trained model) and define the labels you want, and the model will try to fit text to the defined labels. 

They are different tools and should be thought of in that way. Machine learning offers consistency and lower cost, while Gen AI offers flexibility at a higher cost. 

Both approaches have their strengths, and which approach we choose will depend on our requirements and constraints.
